# Examination of Conscience

A reference for a Catholic examination of conscience organised by the Ten Commandments and the Church precepts. The Commandment content is sourced from the USCCB's "A Brief Examination of Conscience Based on the Ten Commandments".

## Language

**Offense**:
A kind of wrong listed under a Commandment, such as "Willful doubt of any article of Faith". An Offense is not an Act: it is what an Act may match. An Offense belongs to a Commandment and may also be in one Offense category under it; grouping is optional. Where the source gives one list for several Commandments (the Seventh and Tenth), each Commandment has its own Offenses with that wording. No two Offenses of one Commandment share wording. Offenses are unordered.
_Avoid_: Sin

**Act**:
Something a particular penitent did or failed to do, such as stealing from their parents. One Act may match Offenses under several Commandments: stealing from one's parents matches an Offense against the Fourth and one against the Seventh. Acts are not recorded.
_Avoid_: Sin, offense

**Offense category**:
A named grouping of a Commandment's Offenses, such as "Those against Faith". A category belongs to exactly one Commandment and always has at least one Offense. A category labels a group and never names a wrongful act: a source heading that names an act, such as "Neglect to hear Mass on Sundays and Holy days of Obligation", is an Offense. No two categories of one Commandment share a name. Categories are unordered. Where the source lists a Commandment's Offenses with no sub-headings, those Offenses are in no category.
_Avoid_: Sin type, offense group

**Examination question**:
An ordered prompt a penitent asks themselves about a Commandment or a Church precept. Each question is about exactly one of these, and questions are ordered within it. It is independent of Offenses: a question is not linked to the Offenses it covers.
_Avoid_: Prompt, checklist item

**Commandment**:
One of the Ten Commandments in the Catholic numbering, identified by number 1–10 (the Ninth and Tenth are the two commandments against coveting). Other traditions' numberings are out of scope. It owns its Offenses, Offense categories and Examination questions.
_Avoid_: Rule, precept

**Church precept**:
One of five obligations the Church places on Catholics, such as "To confess one's sins at least once a year", identified by number 1–5. It is not a Commandment, even where the two cover similar ground. It owns its Examination questions.
_Avoid_: Precept (on its own), commandment of the Church, rule
