# Examination of Conscience

A reference for a Catholic examination of conscience organised by the Ten Commandments, sourced from the USCCB's "A Brief Examination of Conscience Based on the Ten Commandments".

## Language

**Offense**:
A specific wrongful act or omission against a Commandment, such as "Willful doubt of any article of Faith". An Offense belongs to exactly one Offense category; an act that offends two Commandments is recorded as a separate Offense under each, with no shared identity. Offenses are unordered.
_Avoid_: Sin

**Offense category**:
A named grouping of Offenses under one or more Commandments, such as "Those against Faith". A category serves more than one Commandment only where the source gives one list for several (the Seventh and Tenth share one). Categories are unordered. Where the source lists a Commandment's Offenses with no sub-headings, they sit under a single category named "General".
_Avoid_: Sin type, offense group

**Examination question**:
An ordered prompt a penitent asks themselves about a Commandment. It is independent of Offenses: a question is not linked to the Offenses it covers.
_Avoid_: Prompt, checklist item

**Commandment**:
One of the Ten Commandments in the Catholic numbering, identified by number 1–10 (the Ninth and Tenth are the two commandments against coveting). Other traditions' numberings are out of scope. It owns Examination questions and is linked to Offense categories (a category may be shared with another Commandment).
_Avoid_: Rule, precept
