# Examination of Conscience

A reference for a Catholic examination of conscience organised by the Ten Commandments, sourced from the USCCB's "A Brief Examination of Conscience Based on the Ten Commandments".

## Language

**Offense**:
A specific wrongful act or omission against a Commandment, such as "Willful doubt of any article of Faith". An Offense belongs to exactly one Offense category; an act that offends two Commandments is recorded as a separate Offense under each, with no shared identity.
_Avoid_: Sin

**Offense category**:
A named grouping of Offenses under one Commandment, such as "Those against Faith". Categories are unordered.
_Avoid_: Sin type, offense group

**Examination question**:
An ordered prompt a penitent asks themselves about a Commandment. It is independent of Offenses: a question is not linked to the Offenses it covers.
_Avoid_: Prompt, checklist item

**Commandment**:
One of the Ten Commandments in the Catholic numbering, identified by number 1–10 (the Ninth and Tenth are the two commandments against coveting). Other traditions' numberings are out of scope. It owns Examination questions and Offense categories.
_Avoid_: Rule, precept
